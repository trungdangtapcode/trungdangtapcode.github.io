# University campus detail library v1

This source-art packet adds a contemporary university vocabulary to the existing environment
library. It is intended for a courtyard, library threshold and academic study terrace. It does
not replace Service Court or implement a Black Tide/Last Shift campus mission.

## Artwork and reuse

Four original transparent PNG sheets contain 36 separately registered designs:

| Family | New designs | Placement purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student belongings | 9 | Books, notes, bags and study equipment explain where people were working. |
| Courtyard fixtures | 9 | Benches, bike racks, campus directory, notices and services define gathering places and route edges. |
| Campus grounds | 9 | Planters, low foliage and restrained ground details break up paving without filling circulation lanes. |
| Academic furnishings | 9 | Book handling, lecture and laboratory equipment give different pockets a recognizable function. |

The intact/worn bench and upright/dropped backpack are authored silhouette/state alternatives,
not procedural recolors or a destruction simulation. Existing desks, chairs, lockers, shelves,
papers and service objects remain useful and unchanged. Do not regenerate them solely to raise
the asset count. The review's reuse inventory records exact source paths and hashes.

The canonical catalog and native registration are in
[props_import.json](../../../content/art/authoring/campus_v1/props_import.json). Each entry owns
its stable asset ID, explicit Tiled tile ID, reviewed source cell, native dimensions, pivot,
support/contact guide and classification. Preserve existing tile IDs when adding assets;
append a new ID rather than deriving identity from array position.

## Sources and native export

`source/` retains the four original GPT Image PNGs unchanged. `prompts/` retains the exact
English prompts, and [generation.json](generation.json) records generation IDs and SHA256
hashes. Generation used the built-in GPT Image tool, not an API/CLI fallback. New artwork has
no institution names, logos or plot-specific lettering. Reused artwork keeps its own provenance.

Run from the repository root:

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/assets/campus/export_assets.ps1
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/assets/campus/build_study.ps1
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/assets/campus/export_gallery.ps1
```

Native PNGs, receipts, Tiled study files and renders are generated under
`build/campus_asset_review/`; they are not committed. The exporter reuses the existing native
crop/nearest-neighbor workflow and shared palette quantizer. Its declared recipe preserves
aspect ratio, adds transparent padding, bottom-aligns, thresholds alpha and maps colors to the
project palette. This is export of authored pixels, not runtime construction of props.

The study reuses the material atlas exported by
`tools/assets/export_palette_tiles.ps1 -StylePath content/maps/material_review/native_style.json`,
the installed portable Tiled rasterizer, and the registered Survivor definition/atlas under
`build/bin/Release/assets/demo/`. These are explicit prerequisites, not regenerated by the study
builder. The actor source paths, selected clip/direction/pose and expected scale are authored in
`content/art/authoring/campus_v1/placement.json`; missing inputs produce an error.

The composition has 48 prop placements, including seven instances of five existing furniture
assets. Four small tabletop details are supported by two desk instances. The separate catalog
retains all 36 new assets and a lossless crop of the registered south idle Survivor for scale.
This composition is deliberately a dressing study: it contains no university building shell,
finished lawn/terrain transition bank or mission route.

Source sheets are enlarged authoring art; review the exported 1x sprites before approving
detail quality. Sizes are calibrated against the current Survivor's 48-pixel visible height
inside its 96x64 frame, independently of camera zoom. Source-sheet detail is not evidence that
an 8-pixel cup or 16-pixel book is readable in gameplay.

## Placement contract

- Freestanding objects use a bottom-center anchor. Contact rectangles are placement guides;
  this decorative packet has null gameplay collision. Do not derive blockers from alpha.
- Floor scatter belongs below actors/standing objects. Tabletop items need an authored support
  position and layer; the library does not infer elevation or attach them to a desk automatically.
- Use shrubs, planters and fixtures to frame paths. Keep the central connected lane quiet;
  papers, leaves and study belongings belong in small contextual clusters, not uniform scatter.
- The path bollard is an unlit fixture. Real illumination requires the existing light-content
  interface. Directory graphics are environmental marks, not a functioning minimap or objective.
- Book returns, cases and lab objects are decorative until an integration owner gives them
  explicit interaction/loot definitions. Their appearance does not establish a new mechanic.

The editable Tiled study is a placement proof for this art library. Its generated map is not
the canonical mission map. Retain edits as new authoring input before regenerating a study;
an accepted campus level must enter the existing source-owned Tiled map/resource workflow.

## Review scope

The source brief is [here](../../../planning/contracts/UNIVERSITY_CAMPUS_ASSET_BRIEF_2026-11-07.md).
Acceptance requires the actual PNG exports, hash/reference checks and native-scale composition
