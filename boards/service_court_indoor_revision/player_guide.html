Build and screenshots · Play

Restore power. Get through the gates. Reach the coast.

Press Enter to start. Move with WASD. Hold E near the active control; releasing, taking damage or moving away cancels an unfinished hold. The mission continues on one map, carrying your health, ammunition and inventory.

  1. Enter from the south. Go through the compound opening and head west to the electrical workshop. Its main entrance is on the south side.
  2. Restore power. Operate the workshop control. The noise brings more zombies. You can leave through the main entrance or its east service door.
  3. Clear security. Enter the large eastern building from the south lobby. The office and control are to the west; the long eastern corridor leads to the archive. Deal with the defenders in the security complex, then release the lock at the office control.
  4. Return to Gate A. The archive has a west exit into the yard. You can also return through the south lobby and circle the building. Start the motor at the northern gate. Yard lamps switch on and the heavy attack begins. Keep moving while the motor works.
  5. Cross and close it. Pass north through the open gate, then hold E at the far-side control to close it behind you. Crossing this gate does not finish the mission.
  6. Choose the next route. Take the direct loading lane or the west service lane toward Gate B. Activate its motor, survive the wait and cross north.
  7. Reach the coast alive. Continue to coastal extraction to win. There is no third gate-defense stage.
Desks, shelves, cases and freight currently furnish the location; they are not loot containers. Environment art, route discovery and combat pacing are still under review.
