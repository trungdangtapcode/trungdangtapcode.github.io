# Service Court tile composition plan

**Scope:** bounded visual reuse study for the current 96 x 112 map (32 px cells). Coordinates below are world pixels unless marked as tile columns/rows. This plan adds no art, collision, or gameplay objects. Keep mission controls, collision rectangles, and smoke waypoints fixed while trying visual-only placements. Any new blocker/cover must be proposed and validated separately.

## Why the map reads emptier than the concept

The concept image, `build/escape_map_design/escape_route_concept.png`, composes the court from large, connected forms: a south arrival framed by frontage, distinct utility/security rooms, an obvious cargo mass, a broad gate apron, and perimeter structure/lighting. The current map review, `build/escape_route_map_concept_review/service_court.png`, is mostly one uninterrupted hardstand rectangle over asphalt. Its only cargo grouping is six 32 px crates over a 96 x 64 collider; the warehouse sits at the far west edge, while the two matching 320 x 256 room cutaways sit at the far east/west edges. The gate opening is visible, but the barrier art reads as two isolated lines rather than a framed apron. The concept details cannot be faithfully reproduced from the runtime-ready court catalog. There is a forklift candidate at `art/environment/escape_loading_landmark_v1/native/forklift_96.png` and proposed 3x3-cell placement notes, but its README explicitly marks it unaccepted and not integrated into Tiled/runtime; the car-state image at `art/environment/props/car_states_source.png` is only a source sheet, with no map tileset or runtime placement contract. No reusable shipping-container asset or matching tank/pipe/complete gate-jamb kit is registered for this map. Do not place these as if they were approved content.

## Vehicle and container inventory check

The forklift is a visual study only and is not map-ready. The car-state sheet is not an exported sprite set. The active map registers props, controls, industrial facade modules, coastal set, lights and service-room cutaways, but no forklift, car, or container tileset/IDs. Current-map asset choices therefore exclude these candidates; root may track their acceptance/integration separately.

## Likely improvement order and limits

1. Establish four readable ground zones with the current asphalt/concrete palette: south arrival pad, west service yard, east checkpoint threshold, and Gate A apron. Keep paving transitions broad and geometric rather than adding lane markings.
2. Consolidate existing silhouettes: west warehouse + west room, east checkpoint room, cargo stack + pad, and Gate A + lamps + motor station. Do not add loose props merely to increase density.
3. Keep both 96 px bypasses and the authored objective route clear in the preview. If stronger cargo cover, visible perimeter returns, or service equipment are needed after this pass, they require either deliberate collision changes or new matching authored art, tracked as separate work.

This is a placement proposal based on the two lossy inspection previews and current Tiled metadata. It is not an in-game visual acceptance or route-safety result.
## Blocking additions (separate decision, not proposed here)

The six existing cargo sprites are decorative over one 96 x 64 collider; the warehouse visual's collision comes from a separate authored rectangle. Enlarging either cargo island or adding crate stacks as hard blockers would change the two bypasses and player/zombie paths, so keep all added art nonblocking for this composition pass. If cover is later requested, author its collider footprint explicitly and recheck the objective smoke, both loops, room doorway widths, Gate A crossing, and far-side close-control approach before adopting it.
