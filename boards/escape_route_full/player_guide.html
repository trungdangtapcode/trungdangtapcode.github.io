Build and evidence · Play this candidate · Selected court concept

Get through the gates. Reach the coast.

This runtime slice continues through one world. Crossing Gate A opens the next stage; it does not win or load a new map. Your health, ammunition and inventory carry forward.

Press Enter to start. Move with WASD and hold E near the active control. Releasing, taking damage or leaving cancels an unfinished hold. After activation, the gate motor continues while you move and fight.

  1. Restore power. Enter the west service room through its south doorway and operate the control. The objective HUD identifies the active task.
  2. Release security. Cross to the east service room and use its control.
  3. Open Gate A. Reach the console beside the north gate. Start its motor, survive the opening cycle, then physically cross north through the passage.
  4. Choose a loading route. Follow the direct lane north or take the longer west service lane. Both lead to the freight yard without resetting the encounter.
  5. Open Gate B. Activate the next console and cross the gate when it opens. Pressure follows mission events; there is no requirement to clear every zombie.
  6. Reach coastal extraction. Continue north to the marked destination alive. Arrival wins. There is no third hold encounter.
Current limits: this is a playable route integration, not accepted final environment art. The court still needs stronger warehouse/workshop framing, a cargo island with two readable bypasses, visible walls joined to the gate barriers and purposeful work-zone surfaces. Human route discovery, survival balance and performance on the full route remain separate review gates.
