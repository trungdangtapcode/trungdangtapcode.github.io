Power. Security. Gate. Escape.

The service yard exit is isolated. Restore power in the west maintenance room, release security in the east room, then start Gate A. You win by crossing the gate and reaching the exit north of it. There is no kill quota.

Start the encounter with Enter. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse and hold E at the current console. This is the first court only; it does not include the whole planned escape map.

Durations describe this candidate's encounter configuration, not fixed engine values. Releasing E, leaving a control or being hit cancels an unfinished hold; it does not undo completed objectives.

What to judge while playing

Can you find each room from the paving and objective text? Are the console holds readable under pressure? Does the cross-court trip expose you to a meaningful risk? Is the motor wait too quiet or too long? Technical route tests do not answer these player-experience questions.

Existing art is retained. The new consoles reuse authored PowerStation frames and the route reuses paving tiles. This increment provides a playable objective sequence; it does not claim finished set dressing, full-map content or human playtest acceptance.
