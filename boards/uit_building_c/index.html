Building C / three-floor campus

Editable Tiled authoring slice. Three aligned floors, with thirty two-door teaching rooms and twelve separate washrooms. Each floor has west men's/women's rooms to the left of the shared west stair, with north/south entrance routes, plus two compact northeast washrooms. The southeast teaching room retains two doors; its eastern corridor wall holds student-club equipment. Gender assignments are recorded in the room register; dedicated doorway pictogram art is not supplied. This board is rendered art and design data; it is not a playable multi-floor mission.

Camera: orthogonal RPG three-quarter, with tall north wall faces, solid side returns and short foreground wall faces. The earlier nadir/floor-plan wall presentation is rejected. A native Survivor reference is shown inside a room and in the gallery on each floor. These are scale guides, not live actors.

Current user layout: eight original classroom bays to the left and two to the right of the offset connector: four large rooms west and one east per wing. The north stair meets the floral gallery with empty surrounds on all floors; the west stair shares its block with toilets on the left, a route toward Building E above and the sports-lawn route below. The ground-floor west lawn has mature modular trees, the east zone has an ornamental pond and landscaped artificial turf, and the central event/study court uses floral tiles. South of the building are flowerbeds, asphalt road and a grass soccer pitch; stone paving is limited to the east garden walk. Rooms have north and foreground windows where walls do not adjoin another room or door. Upper-floor courtyards and outdoor grounds are voids. The normal wall tiles are now the original GPT Image school-wall packet, not the earlier LPC material study.
