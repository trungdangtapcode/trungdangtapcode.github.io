Story and required route

Emergency security has sealed the district. A pursuing survivor starts the local gate motor, stays mobile during its noisy cycle, then physically crosses the opened gate.

South arrival → optional preparation → Gate A control → opening under pressure → through Gate A → loading lanes → Gate B → waterfront extraction.

Both court bypasses meet before Gate A; neither skips it. No kill quota opens the gate. See the current implementation comparison for the authored route and unresolved layout gaps.

Current implementation comparison · 1 November 2026

The current Tiled layout review includes the Service Court and Freight Yard crops. These are authoring renders, not art, visual, or gameplay acceptance.

  • The west warehouse and small east room read as detached buildings, not the intended west warehouse and east workshop frontages.
