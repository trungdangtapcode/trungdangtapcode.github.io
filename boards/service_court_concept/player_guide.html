Review index · Launch candidate

Enter. Restore. Secure. Defend. Close. Continue.

Follow the six beats in the selected Service Court A runtime profile. The route proceeds from the south court through Gate A and continues in the same world toward Gate B and the coast.

Review status: pending technical and native evidence. This guide describes the authored route and controls; it does not establish a successful run, art acceptance, or human playtest acceptance.

Controls

Press Enter to start the encounter. Move with WASD, sprint with Shift, aim with the mouse, and hold E at a control to interact. LMB fires; P pauses. Releasing E, leaving the control, or taking damage interrupts an unfinished hold.

Six beats

BeatRoute and actionConsequence
1. Enter from the southStart the encounter and move into the court. Read the two room entrances and the cargo-island loops while the chase remains active.
2. Restore west-room powerEnter the west electrical room and hold E at its control. Interruption cancels progress. Completing restoration gives an audible cue and increases pursuit pressure.
3. Release east-room securityCross the court, enter the east security room, and clear its local clearance area. Hold E at the control. Living zombies in that room block release; this is a local threat check, not a whole-map kill quota.
4. Start Gate AReach the outdoor control south of Gate A and hold E. The perimeter lamps activate and a larger wave pressures the court. The motor runs while you move and defend; eliminating every zombie is not required.
5. Cross and close Gate ACross north through the open passage. From its far side, reach the close control and hold E. The passage cannot close while occupied. Completing the close restores the blocker and stops pursuit through that route. Opening alone is not completion.
6. Continue into the loading lanesMove onward through the same world toward Gate B, then the coastal exit. Health, ammunition, inventory, enemies, equipment, and session identity continue. Closing Gate A completes this court beat, not the full escape.

The implementation contract records encounter timings and evidence criteria in the runtime contract. Timings are encounter data and may change with the candidate.

What remains to review

Check whether entrances, both loops and controls are findable; whether the security-room block is explained; whether the Gate A defense and close interaction feel fair; and whether the transition into the later loading lanes is clear.
